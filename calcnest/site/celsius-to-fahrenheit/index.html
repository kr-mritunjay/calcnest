How to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (1.8), then add 32.

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32

For example, normal body temperature of 37°C is 98.6°F, and the boiling point of water, 100°C, is 212°F.

Mental shortcut: double the Celsius value and add 30. For 20°C: 40 + 30 = 70°F (exact: 68°F). It gets less accurate at extreme temperatures.

Frequently asked questions

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

At −40. That is the only point where both scales show the same number.

What is room temperature in Fahrenheit?

Around 20 to 25°C, which is 68 to 77°F.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9. For 98.6°F: (98.6 − 32) × 5/9 = 37°C.
