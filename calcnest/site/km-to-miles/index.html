How to convert kilometres to miles

Multiply the number of kilometres by 0.621371, or divide it by 1.609344.

miles = kilometres × 0.621371

For example, a marathon is 42.195 km, which is 26.219 miles.

Mental shortcut: multiply by 0.6 for a quick estimate. 50 km × 0.6 = 30 miles, close to the exact 31.07.

Frequently asked questions

How many miles are in 1 kilometre?

1 km is 0.621371 miles.

How many kilometres are in a mile?

1 mile is exactly 1.609344 km.

Does this work for km/h to mph?

Yes. Speed uses the same factor, so 100 km/h is about 62.14 mph.
