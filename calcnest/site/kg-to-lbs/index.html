How to convert kg to lbs

Multiply the weight in kilograms by 2.20462.

pounds = kilograms × 2.20462

For example, 70 kg is 154.32 lbs.

Mental shortcut: double the kilograms, then add 10% of that. For 50 kg: 100 + 10 = 110 lbs (exact: 110.23).

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in 1 kg?

1 kg is about 2.20462 pounds.

Why is pounds written as lb?

The abbreviation comes from the Latin word libra. 'lbs' is the plural form commonly used in writing.

How do I convert pounds to kg?

Divide by 2.20462, or multiply by 0.453592. Use the swap button to do it automatically.
