The three formulas

Percentage means "out of 100". These are the formulas behind the three calculators above.

Percentage of a number

Result = (percentage ÷ 100) × number

15% of 2,400 is 0.15 × 2,400 = 360.

One number as a percentage of another

Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100

45 out of 60 is 45 ÷ 60 × 100 = 75%.

Percentage change

Change = ((new − old) ÷ old) × 100

A price moving from 200 to 250 is a 25% increase. Going back from 250 to 200 is a 20% decrease. The two are not equal because the starting value differs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Use the first calculator to find the discount amount (discount % of the price), then subtract it from the price. A 20% discount on 1,500 saves 300, so you pay 1,200.

Why is a 25% increase not reversed by a 25% decrease?

Because the second percentage applies to a bigger number. After going up 25% from 200 to 250, you need a 20% drop, not 25%, to get back to 200.

Can the result be more than 100%?

Yes. If the part is larger than the whole, or a value more than doubles, the percentage goes past 100.
